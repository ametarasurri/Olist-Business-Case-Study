# Olist E-Commerce — Business Case Study

A consulting-style business case study built on 
real Olist e-commerce transaction data from 2016–2018.

The analysis identifies five key business problems 
and provides strategic recommendations with projected 
revenue impact of approximately $5.9M annually.

## Key Findings
- 3.12% repeat rate vs 25–30% industry benchmark
- São Paulo drives ~37% of revenue — concentration risk
- ~53% freight-to-price ratio in home comfort category
- Delivery speed does not drive customer satisfaction
- Combined recommendation impact: ~$5.9M incremental GMV
